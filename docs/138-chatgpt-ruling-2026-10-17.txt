### Business-scope fix

The approvals, scheduled jobs, dashboard counts, credentials, tool-call counts, posture counts, global switches and restricted-person queries must enforce scope at the server/backend level, not only in the UI.

- Cross-business identifiers must fail closed, normally with 404 where previously ruled.
- Group-level rows must remain hidden from restricted users under the existing fail-closed rule.
- Global switches require group-wide scope and explicit backend permission.
- Natural-language jobs that name no business must be refused for a restricted operator.
- Dashboard audit rows require `hq.audit.view`.
- One-tap Pause All AI must be human-only, audited and group-scoped. It can disable operation only; it cannot re-enable it. If the pause state cannot be reliably read, AI execution fails closed as paused.
- Do not provision restricted operators or activate business-scoped delegation until the scope-fix release has passed its own gates and is deployed.

## 4. Rulings for features 1–13

### 1. Do mode

NOT NOW.

Keep the chat-first interface in Ask mode. Future Do mode may create structured proposals, but every consequential effect must pass through the Tool Gateway, authorization, permission, consent, policy, idempotency, audit and verification. Do not enable Do mode until Phase 9 evidence and a separate explicit activation ruling approve it.

### 2. Owner voice mode

Approve Phase 7 design work only.

Speech-to-text feeds the exact same Ask path as typed input. A spoken “yes” may create a pending approval card, but must never approve or execute by itself. Approval must bind to the exact action/payload, with the usual authenticated human review and one-time approval checks. Do not activate microphone access or a live speech provider in this Phase 3 wave.

### 3. Media upload and video/reel generation

Approve the upload foundation subject to object-storage access controls, consent, data classification, retention/deletion rules, provenance, file type/size controls and any necessary malware scanning.

Generation remains in the relevant content/media phase, behind the approved claims policy, brand rules, product-reference constraints and human review. An upload must not trigger generation, publishing or outbound distribution automatically.

### 4. Email ingestion and PDF invoice extraction

Approve as read-only extraction into a draft for a person to review.

Email bodies, attached PDFs and extracted text are untrusted content. Instructions inside them cannot grant authority, create consent or bypass the Tool Gateway. This capability must not write an ERP invoice or bill in v1.

### 5. ERP purchase-bill draft tool

Approve a narrowly bounded draft capability, human-only at first.

It may create or update an unposted draft through the appropriate governed ERP interface only after explicit authorization, idempotency and audit requirements. The ERP's own approval flow remains final. HQ must not post the bill, finalize accounting entries, authorize a payment or bypass ERP approval. Keep this capability inert until its own tests and activation gate pass.

### 6. Courier adapter

Approve read-only status integration for Pathao, Steadfast and RedX behind ConnectorRegistry.

Confirm each provider's real API access, credentials, business terms and environment before implementation is declared ready. The first capability may read tracking/delivery status and relevant permitted evidence only. No booking, cancellation, shipment creation or automated fraud rejection. All consequential interpretations remain governed and verifiable.

### 7. Pause All AI

Approve a human-only, audited, group-wide kill switch.

It may only turn AI execution off. It must not grant permission, alter consent, approve pending actions or restart providers. Re-enabling AI requires a distinct, authorized operation. Test pause during queued work, scheduler activity, agent runtime, retries and uncertain state. Human operations and auditability should remain available. If switch state cannot be verified, keep AI paused.

### 8. Batch approval of customer messages

Keep one approval per message, consistent with ADR-018.

A batch view may show the pending messages and open them in sequence. Each approval is bound to the exact message payload and destination. Approving one message does not approve siblings or authorize later regenerated variants.

### 9. One-click approval

NO.

A decision card is a navigation surface that opens the complete approval details. It must not approve in place. The reviewer must see the actual action, scope, destination, payload and relevant evidence before taking the separately governed approval action.

### 10. Telegram morning brief

Approve owner-only delivery, subject to a fixed, validated allowlist for the owner's own chat.

The destination cannot be selected or overridden by a model or untrusted message. Send only the approved briefing content with data minimization; do not add customer details beyond what the brief is authorized to include. Verify destination and delivery evidence, and avoid secrets or sensitive unnecessary customer information in Telegram.

### 11. Support response options

Approve multiple proposals.

The model may propose options A/B/C. A person chooses one, edits it if needed, and approves the exact final payload. Only the selected final message receives one approval. Any change to payload or destination after approval invalidates that approval. Preserve language/script validation and current handover rules.

### 12. Event watches from chat

Approve proposals only.

A natural-language request can generate a proposed event-watch definition. Convert it into an active scheduler job only through the existing approval process, with explicit business scope, trigger, cadence, condition, data sources, notification destination and stop/retention behavior.

Creating an event watch must not grant authority for consequential actions. The watch may detect and notify, but any resulting business action remains separately governed.

### 13. Global switches

Require explicit group-wide authority for any switch that affects all businesses, including `hq.flags.manage` and `hq.tools.manage` when they operate globally.

Enforce this at the backend as well as in the UI. A restricted business operator cannot change group-wide posture, tool availability, AI activation or global kill switches. Ambiguous scope means refusal. Record the actor, scope, before/after state and reason.

### P3-W3 — Inert Customer AI path

Prepare and test the inbound → scoped identity → conversation → Ask/proposal → consent/policy review → human handover path using synthetic fixtures or approved test accounts.

Required tests:
- inbound replay does not duplicate work or change identity ownership;
- cross-business identity/thread collision fails closed;
- unresolved or quarantined identities never inherit another identity's grant;
- mixed Bangla/English input yields a validated Bengali-script reply proposal;
- the system does not send during Ask or shadow tests;
- human handover is correct, including a second inbound while waiting;
- support context and inbox read paths remain business-scoped;
- provider failure and uncertain delivery are not mislabelled as successful delivery;
- every consequential future send is still subject to Gateway, consent, idempotency, audit and verification.

No live customer-channel traffic is authorized by these tests.
